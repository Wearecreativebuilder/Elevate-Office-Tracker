How your data is saved

This starter app saves records in your current browser using local storage. Vercel hosts the site, but does not store or synchronize financial entries by itself.

For shared use across office members and devices, connect an authenticated database (such as Supabase) before treating this as the official shared ledger.

Export regular backups. Clearing browser storage or switching devices may otherwise lose access to these entries.
